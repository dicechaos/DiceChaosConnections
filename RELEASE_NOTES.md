## Dice Chaos Connections — Official Release (`v1.0.0`)

Welcome to the official **`v1.0.0`** release of **Dice Chaos Connections** by **[Dice Chaos](https://dicechaos.com)**!

**Dice Chaos Connections** is an offline-first TTRPG campaign mind-map, detective conspiracy board, and spoiler-safe **2nd-Screen Live Player Display** built for Dungeon Masters, Game Masters, worldbuilders, and tabletop groups rolling real **[Dice Chaos](https://dicechaos.com)** polyhedral dice around the table.

> **Companion App:** Pair **Dice Chaos Connections** at the game table with **[Dice Chaos Display](https://github.com/dicechaos/DiceChaosDisplay)**—our zero-lag 4K/8K battlemap studio, looping video scene player, feathered Fog of War, and true 1-inch (`25.4 mm`) physical miniature TV calibrator!

### New Since `v1.0.0-beta.1`
- **Unified Editable & Customizable Connection Points:** Edit, slide (`Alt+Drag`), rename, restyle, or hide **all 8 pre-populated connection points** (`Top`, `Right`, `Bottom`, `Left`, `TL`, `TR`, `BL`, `BR`) just like newly added border points—plus right-click any entity or group border to add unlimited custom connection points with attached lines moving in lockstep and 1-click **Reset / Restore Defaults**.
- **Custom Connection Point Visuals:** Customize per-point **Marker Shape** (*Diamond*, *Circle*, *Ring*, *Square*, *Edge Pill*, *Hidden*), **Size** (`S` / `M` / `L`), **Color** (*Match Border* auto-accent + 8 swatches + custom hex), **Visibility** (*Hover*, *DM Studio*, or *Public Map* displayed on the Player Screen & poster exports), **Border Label Badges**, and **Apply Style to All Points**.
- **360° Freeform Line Endpoints & Cross-Node Reconnection:** Drag either end of any selected connection line around any card or group perimeter (`5%–95%` with `25% / 50% / 75%` and port magnetic detents, or hold `Alt` for smooth placement) or across onto another entity/group to reconnect.
- **Configurable Drag Jump Distance & Grid Snap (`G`):** Choose **`Free (1px)`** or **`4px` / `8px` / `16px` / `24px` / `32px` / `48px`** drag steps from the top header bar or canvas right-click menu, hold **`Alt`** while dragging to invert snapping on the fly, and enjoy theme-harmonized background grids across cards, groups, and curve waypoints.

### Full `v1.0.0` Feature Highlights
- **Tactile Infinite Conspiracy & Campaign Canvas:** 1-step node & relationship creation, manual card resizing (`W × H` + batch size matching), overlap-smart layer stacking (`Bring to Front` / `Send to Back`), 7 card shapes, custom background colors with automatic WCAG contrast, 40 status markers, and edge-to-edge ("meta") connections.
- **Connectable Group Regions & Factions (`Ctrl+G`):** Wrap wards, factions, or army hosts with editable perimeter connection points, synchronized member dragging, position locking, custom region tints, crest/banner art, and 1-click **Tidy Grid**.
- **Rich TTRPG Sheets & `[[Wiki-Links]]`:** Built-in + custom categories with structured sheet fields, embedded single-file WebP portraits, separate **Public Lore / DM Secrets / Party Notes**, `[[Aliases]]` & `[[Target|Display]]` autocomplete, automatic rename refactoring, and backlinks.
- **Zero-Spoiler 2nd-Screen Player Window & Home Conspiracy Edition:** Project a sanitized live view to a TV, second monitor, or VTT screen-share with **Spotlight** & **`???` Mystery Portrait** reveals—or export a spoiler-free `.json` handout for players to add **Party Notes & Theory Pins** and merge them non-destructively back into your master map.
- **Session Time Machine:** Save session snapshots, preview past sessions safely, and inspect `+ New`, `~ Changed` (field-level before/after), and `Removed` diffs across checkpoints.
- **Compendium & Poster Exports:** Export **Obsidian Vaults** (YAML frontmatter + `[[Wiki-Links]]` + `assets/`), **Markdown (`.md`)**, **PDF Lorebooks (`.pdf`)**, and **High-DPI Map Posters (`1×`–`3×` `.png`)** ready for printing or loading straight into **[Dice Chaos Display](https://github.com/dicechaos/DiceChaosDisplay)**.
- **4 Tabletop Themes, Smart Auto-Save & Built-In Showcase:** *Modern Studio*, *Dark Arcane*, *Parchment & Ink*, and *Detective Corkboard* themes, crash-safe atomic `.json` saves with a header **Auto-Save** ON/OFF toggle, and a 36-node ***The Hobbit: Eve of Five Armies*** campaign showcase + 5-step **Tour**.
